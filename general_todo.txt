General TODO.

- Customer requests from Roche/DELT, never actioned.
  Source: "20220112 Bernie Magnetic Beads Purification Feedback.pptx", Felix
  Gruber and Quentin Strebel on behalf of the DELT team, 12 Jan 2022, written
  after ten purification runs. Held in the business archive under
  Robot_Arnie_Bernie/Bernie/Customer_Runs/20220118_01/, together with the
  samplesheet and logs they attached.

  None of it reached this repository: the last commit before the 2026 revival
  is 2021-12-20, three weeks before the feedback was written. Two items from
  their list are left out below - the bead aspiration speed, which was a
  question about the right value rather than a request, and the ethanol not
  being fully removed in 2 of 10 runs, which they solved themselves by editing
  eppendorf.json and which is to be judged visually during testing.

  1. Beads dispensed to the wrong tube. Samples declared at positions 1 and 2
     had their beads dispensed to position 0. Their words: "What is wrong?"

     Root cause, confirmed by reading: positionsToPurify() returns the real
     samplesheet positions, e.g. [1, 2], but _returnSampleParameterList()
     returns a dense list indexed 0..n-1. initSamples() (purify.py:355) then
     calls createSamplesToPurifyList() without position_list, so it falls back
     to start_from_position=0 and regenerates positions as [0, 1]. The real
     positions are discarded and replaced by list indices.

     The fix material is already there: positions_to_purify_list is computed at
     purify.py:139 and never used for this, and createSamplesToPurifyList()
     already accepts position_list.

     The same confusion appears a second time and is not in their report.
     Line 315 iterates real positions into _getBeadsVolume(), which reaches
     initial_sample_vol_list[position] - the dense list indexed by a real
     position. With samples at positions 1 and 2 that is an IndexError on a
     two-element list. Roche never hit it because they supplied "Beads volume"
     directly, which takes another path; the "Fraction" path with offset
     positions would crash. Note positionsToPurify2ndStage() uses real
     positions, so the first and second stages disagree on what a position is.

     Worth a unit test over non-contiguous layouts ([1,2], [0,5], [7,8]); no
     hardware needed. Their samplesheet_Delt_2_Pos1-2 makes a ready fixture.

  2. Delay after aspiration lands at the wrong moment. They asked for 3-5 s
     with the tip still in the liquid, immediately after aspiration, so the
     full volume is drawn. What they observed was the delay they set in the csv
     taking effect "after the whole pipetting step".

     Read the code before changing it: time.sleep(pipetting_delay) currently
     does sit right after the plunger reaches zero, tip still immersed. So
     either this was fixed between their run and 2021-12-20, or the cause is
     elsewhere. One candidate worth testing: writeAndWaitCartesian gives up
     after 5 s, so a move that has not physically finished when the sleep
     starts would produce exactly this symptom. Check their attached log first.

  3. Dispense height too high.

     Partly built already, but never committed. The archived working copy of
     bernielib.py, dated 2022-05-02 - after the feedback - carries
     setLiquidDispenseVolLevel / getLiquidDispenseVolLevel and
     dispenseLiquid(calc_insert_vol_as='settings'|'maxvol'), none of which
     exist here. purify.py in that same copy is byte-identical to this one, so
     it was never wired up. Recover that work before rewriting it. Today every
     call site passes the hardcoded extra_vol_insert=100.

  4. Tip touch. Touch the tip to the tube wall after dispensing so the hanging
     droplet is released.

     touchWall() exists and transferLiquid(touch_wall=True) defaults on, but
     purify.py passes touch_wall=False for beads, ethanol and eluent - every
     step they cared about. Wanted as a per-reagent samplesheet parameter
     rather than a hardcoded False.

  5. Pause/break during a run. Genuinely absent: no pause or resume anywhere in
     purify.py or bernielib.py. Pausing between protocol steps is simple and
     safe; mid-step is much harder. Between steps is probably enough.

  6. Timing does not match the samplesheet, and one step is mislabelled.
     Drying time set to 3 min in the csv was not 3 min in the run, and the
     slide notes the step reads as "incubation" where it should say "drying".

  7. Incubation timer starts at the wrong event. It currently starts when the
     beads are added; they argue it should start after mixing completes,
     otherwise every incubation is short by the mixing duration. Changing this
     alters protocol results, so record before/after.

  Also worth knowing when picking this up: the revival project's technical
  backlog lists "implement Roche's request: adjustable pipetting speed" as a
  critical gate. That is already done - the four speed parameters in
  factory_default/samplesheet.csv were added in October 2021, three months
  before the feedback. Roche were asking whether 400 was the right value, not
  for the feature.

- Logging in the test notebooks looks wrong, and runs may not be recorded.
  Affected: 21121701_Bernie_Test.ipynb, jupyter_notebooks/21111801_Bernie_Test.ipynb,
  jupyter_notebooks/21112901_Bernie_Test.ipynb, jupyter_notebooks/21121701_Bernie_Test.ipynb
  and the matching .ipynb_checkpoints copies.

  Each of them starts with:

      import purify
      ...
      logging.basicConfig(filename=(str(time.time())+'purify.log'), level=logging.INFO, ...)
      logging.disable(logging.CRITICAL)

  Two separate problems:

  1. The basicConfig call in the notebook does nothing. Importing purify has
     already configured the root logger, and basicConfig is a no-op once the
     root logger has handlers. So the level and the file name the notebook asks
     for are ignored, and purify's own settings apply instead (DEBUG, plus the
     console handler purify installs).

  2. logging.disable(logging.CRITICAL) then switches off every log record at
     CRITICAL and below, which is all of them. So a purification driven from
     these notebooks writes an essentially empty log file, even though a log
     file is created and looks like a record of the run.

  Worth deciding what the intended behaviour is: if the notebooks are meant to
  keep a record of a run, the disable call should go, and the log setup should
  live in one place rather than being attempted in both purify.py and the
  notebook. Note that purify.py configures logging at import time, which is why
  the notebook cannot override it; see tests/__init__.py for why that import
  time call was left alone rather than moved behind a __main__ guard.
